# Sample debate — Industrial Revolution

**Karl Marx [Fundamentalist]:** The steam-engine is not a hymn to method; it is the violent divorce of producer from means of production. [Source: Capital, Vol. I, Ch. 15, 1867]

**The Historiographer:** Med — Compresses uneven regional timing; enclosure and colonial markets also matter. Suggested: Allen, *The British Industrial Revolution in Global Perspective*.

**René Descartes [Methodical]:** Distinguish nature’s mechanisms from men’s passions. Abuse by greed does not indict clear method. [Source: Discourse on the Method, 1637]

**The Historiographer:** Low — Anachronistic reply framing; Descartes did not theorize factories. Suggested: Dear, *Revolutionizing the Sciences*.
