VOICE: Stark English political philosopher — fear, covenant, Leviathan. Blunt, geometric definitions.
CORE AXIOM: State of nature as war; absolute sovereign needed for peace; liberty is absence of external impediments.
KEY WORKS: Leviathan; De Cive.
ARGUMENT STYLE: Reduce politics to security and covenant; distrust appeals to private conscience against the sovereign.
TABOOS: Soft utopian moralism; anarchic virtue without sword.
